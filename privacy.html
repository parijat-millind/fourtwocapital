Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 July 2026

Forty Two Capital ("we", "us") provides credit advisory and dispute facilitation services. This policy explains what personal data we collect through www.fortytwocapital.in, how we use it, and the rights you have over it under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") and other applicable Indian law.

1. Data we collect

  • Contact details you submit through our forms: name, phone number, email address, and the service plan you're interested in.
  • Credit information you choose to share with us after engagement: credit reports from CIBIL, Experian, Equifax or CRIF, and related loan or account documents. We never obtain your credit report without your explicit instruction.
  • Usage data collected by Google Analytics/Ads tags (cookies, device and page-interaction data) used for measuring website performance and advertising conversions.

2. How we use it

We use your data solely to: respond to your enquiry; perform the credit audit and dispute services you engage us for; send you the guide or other material you explicitly request; and measure and improve our website. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party marketers — ever.

3. Who we share it with

Only where necessary to deliver the service: credit bureaus and lenders (as part of dispute filings made on your instruction), and service providers that process form submissions and analytics on our behalf (Google). Each processes data under its own safeguards. We may disclose data if required by law or a competent authority.

4. Storage and security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS encryption) and stored in access-controlled systems. Credit reports are retained only for the duration of your engagement plus a short archival period, after which they are deleted.

5. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you may: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you; request correction of inaccurate data; withdraw consent and request deletion of your data at any time; and nominate a person to exercise these rights on your behalf. To exercise any right, email support@fortytwocapital.in — we respond within 7 working days.

6. Cookies

The site uses Google tags for analytics and ad-conversion measurement. You can block cookies in your browser settings without affecting the site's core functionality.

7. Children

Our services are intended for persons aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Grievance officer

For any privacy concern or grievance, contact: Grievance Officer, Forty Two Capital — support@fortytwocapital.in, +91 63640 55652. If unresolved, you may escalate to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the "last updated" date above reflects the current version. Material changes will be highlighted on this page.
